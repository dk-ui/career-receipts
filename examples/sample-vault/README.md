# Career vault

> **Sample.** Robin Castellane is fictional, and so are the organizations and every number.
> This shows what a vault looks like a few weeks after setup.

A private, complete record of my working life: every job, project and volunteer role, the
files that prove what I did, and the photos that go with them. **This is the source of
truth.** My CV and review notes read from it, never the other way around.

> Navigating? [index.md](index.md) lists every file. [MANIFEST.md](MANIFEST.md) is the generated map.

## Where things are

| Path | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [profile/](profile/README.md) | Who I am and what I'm aiming for |
| [chapters/](chapters/README.md) | One folder per job or role, each with its evidence and gaps |
| [evidence-ledger.md](evidence-ledger.md) | Every claim on my CV → the file that proves it |
| [cv.md](cv.md) | My live CV, rebuilt from the vault. Every number on it has a ledger row |

A real vault also has `inbox/`, `resumes/`, `cover-letters/` and `job-search/`. The sample
leaves them out to stay short.
