# Quillmont Home Health — Operations Manager

**Status: 🟡 partial** · Jun 2019 – Feb 2022 · Columbus, Ohio

Home health agency sending nurses and therapists to patients' homes. I ran scheduling and
field operations for the central region.

## What I did

Visits were being missed because schedules were built by hand each week and nobody saw a
conflict until a clinician called in. I set up a shared daily conflict report, moved
rescheduling to a two-person desk, and later led the move to a new scheduling system for the
whole clinical staff. My 2021 review is the best record of the results.

## Highlights

1. **Missed home visits down from 9% to 4%.** Proof: my 2021 performance review.
2. **Moved scheduling for 120 clinicians to a new system** without a missed payroll. From
   memory: the project plan and go-live report stayed with the employer.
