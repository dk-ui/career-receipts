# Fernhollow Logistics — Senior Operations Lead

**Status: 🟢 strong** · Mar 2022 – present · Columbus, Ohio (hybrid)

Regional third-party logistics company running warehouses for grocery and home-goods brands.
I run inbound and inventory operations across three of its warehouses.

## What I did

I came in when inbound freight was sitting on the dock for more than a day before it could be
picked. I mapped the receiving flow at each site, found that putaway waited on a single
end-of-shift system update, and moved it to scan-as-you-go with a standard slotting rule.
I wrote the standard, trained the shift leads at all three sites, and ran a weekly review of
the dock-to-stock and mis-pick numbers with each site manager until both held for a full
quarter. Team: 6 shift leads reporting to me, about 90 associates across the sites.

## Highlights

1. **Dock-to-stock time down 39%, from 31 hours to 19 hours, across 3 warehouses.** Proof:
   the dashboard in `media/`.
2. **Mis-picks down 22%** after the slotting standard. Proof: the trend chart in `media/`.
3. **About $410K a year less overtime.** Derived, see the math below.
